How faithful is this? SpaceX has not released Raptor 3 CAD. The exterior is traced from SpaceX's August 2024 photos of Raptor 3 SN1 and scaled to the published ≈3.1 m × ≈1.3 m envelope. Everything hidden (preburners, turbine layout, injector pattern, cooling channels, inner nozzle contour) is an engineering reconstruction. Colour dots in the parts list show which is which.

Raptor 1 was covered in external plumbing and sensors; Raptor 2 merged valves, turned flanges into welds and deleted main-chamber igniters; Raptor 3 moved most secondary plumbing and sensors inside the engine structure, added regen cooling to exposed parts, and dropped the heat shield — which is why the vehicle-side mass fell from 2,875 kg to 1,720 kg.1,6
